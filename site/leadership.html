COMPANY // COMMAND

Command
structure.

Aranruth is organized as directorates, not departments. Authority follows the program, and the program follows the operator. This page describes structure and governance — deliberately, not biographies.

01 // Doctrine

Structure over
personalities.

Companies that run on founder celebrity break when the founder is wrong. Defense companies cannot afford that failure mode. Aranruth publishes its command structure — the chains of authority, review gates, and escalation paths — rather than personality profiles. The people holding these positions are cleared professionals; their identities are shared with counterparties at program level, not on a public page.

What matters to a government customer is answerability: who signs the acceptance test, who owns the failure review, who is reachable at 0300 when a fielded line goes down. Those are roles in a structure, and the structure is below.

02 // Directorates

Five
directorates.

DIRECTORATE // SENSING

Sensing Systems

Owns SELIXOR volumetric occupancy and ARGUS multi-spectral vision: sensor selection, edge-inference models, track integrity, and classification doctrine. Signs every perception acceptance test.

DIRECTORATE // SPECTRUM

Spectrum Operations

Owns WRAITH: DRFM architecture, FPGA signal chains, autonomous management policy, and the spectrum-discipline curriculum delivered to partner signal sections.

DIRECTORATE // DENIAL

Acoustic Denial

Owns BANSHEE: passive array geometry, BPF classifier training, effector integration, and the FOB / MRAP / PACK deployment kits.

DIRECTORATE // THE VOLUME

Proving Ground Command

Owns the Mapimí basin: range scheduling, instrumentation calibration, live-spectrum test cards, and guest-unit escort protocol. Nothing ships without this directorate's validation stamp.

DIRECTORATE // PROGRAMS

Program Integration

Owns the customer interface: intake, requirements translation, fielding plans, sustainment contracts, and the 0300 escalation line for fielded systems.

DIRECTORATE // SECURITY

Security & Assurance

Owns personnel reliability, counterintelligence posture, export-control compliance, and the classified-adjacent information boundary that governs everything we publish — including this page.

03 // Governance

Review gates,
not opinions.

Every system moves through fixed gates: bench validation (subsystem proof on the build floor), Volume validation (full-scale test under live spectrum at the basin), partner exercise (operation by real formations, not our engineers), and fielding authority (signed by the owning directorate and counter-signed by Security & Assurance). A gate failure stops the program. There is no override path that skips the basin.

Engineering review is weekly and adversarial by design: the challenging directorate is assigned, not volunteered. The question in every review is the same — what did the system do when it was supposed to fail?
