Describe the

Aranruth briefs government, defense and law-enforcement programs directly. No channel partners, no resellers, no procurement theater. This page is the single open channel into the program office — tell us the operational problem, not the product name.

01 // Channels

Who we
brief.

Defense programs — defense@aranruth.com. Ministry and armed-forces program offices, joint C-UAS cells, spectrum-warfare commands. Briefings are conducted at the technical level: requirements, integration constraints, employment doctrine, then live serials at the basin under controlled conditions.

Latin America sales — ventas@aranruth.com. Government agencies, border institutions, critical-infrastructure operators across the region. Spanish-language technical channel for procurement, pilot design and training serials.

Careers — careers@aranruth.com. Engineering and prior-service intake. See the careers and veteran-careers pages for disciplines and MOS mapping.

All three channels terminate in the program office, which controls which directorate answers and when. That is deliberate: the person who understands the failure mode should be the one who talks to you, not a sales layer that understands nothing.

02 // Intake rules

Channel
discipline.

Open channel only. Nothing classified, target-specific, or operationally sensitive through this form or these email addresses — ever. Submission does not create authorization, procurement status, or access clearance. If your requirement lives at a classified level, say so at the program level and the Security & Assurance directorate will open the appropriate channel through government-to-government processes.

Problem-first intake. The program office routes by operational problem, not product interest. A legacy system failing at your perimeter says more than a product name ever could. Describe the environment, the failure, the operational consequence, and what you have already tried.

Response discipline. Intake is triaged weekly and answered by the owning directorate. Evaluation paths, basin serials and integration conversations follow from the technical fit — not from seniority of sender.
